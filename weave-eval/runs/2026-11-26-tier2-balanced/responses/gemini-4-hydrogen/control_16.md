***Project Hail Mary* by Andy Weir** is a great pick—it's a fast-paced, clever survival story about a lone astronaut trying to save Earth using science and an unexpected friendship.

If you're in the mood for something else:
- **Epic world-building:** *Dune* by Frank Herbert
- **Mind-bending mystery:** *The Three-Body Problem* by Liu Cixin
- **Warm and character-driven:** *The Long Way to a Small, Angry Planet* by Becky Chambers

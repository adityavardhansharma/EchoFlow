For most healthy adults, moderate coffee consumption (about 3 to 4 cups a day) is not bad for you and is actually linked to lower risks of type 2 diabetes, heart disease, and certain neurodegenerative conditions.

However, too much caffeine can cause jitters, anxiety, acid reflux, or disrupted sleep, especially if drunk late in the day. People who are pregnant, sensitive to caffeine, or have certain heart or blood-pressure conditions should limit their intake or choose decaf.

1. Place the eggs gently in a single layer at the bottom of a pot and cover them with cold water by about 2–3 cm.
2. Bring the water to a rolling boil over medium-high heat, then cover the pot and turn off the heat (or reduce to a very gentle simmer).
3. Let the eggs sit for **6 minutes** for soft-boiled (runny yolk), **8 minutes** for medium, or **10–12 minutes** for hard-boiled.
4. Transfer the eggs immediately to a bowl of ice water for at least 5 minutes before peeling.

In everyday writing:

- **Affect** is usually a **verb** meaning *to influence or change something* ("The rain will affect our plans").
- **Effect** is usually a **noun** meaning *a result or outcome* ("The rain had a big effect on our plans").

A quick trick: **A**ffect is an **A**ction; **E**ffect is an **E**nd result. (Less commonly, *effect* can be a verb meaning "to bring about," and *affect* can be a psychology noun for observable emotion.)

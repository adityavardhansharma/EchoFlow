**6 months before:** Set budget and guest list; book venue and key vendors.
**5 months:** Choose outfits; confirm photographer and caterer.
**4 months:** Send save-the-dates; plan ceremony and décor.
**3 months:** Order invitations; arrange travel and accommodation.
**2 months:** Send invitations; confirm menus and music.
**1 month:** Finalise guest count, seating, fittings and vendor schedule.
**1 week:** Reconfirm suppliers, pack essentials and delegate day-of tasks.
**Day before:** Rehearsal, collect outfits and get some rest.
**Wedding day:** Eat breakfast, follow the schedule, and enjoy the celebration.
